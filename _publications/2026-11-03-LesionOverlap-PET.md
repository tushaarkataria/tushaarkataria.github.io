excerpt: "ABSTRACT PENDING (summary below is a stand-in, not the verbatim arXiv abstract). Metabolic tumor volume (MTV) derived from automated PET lesion segmentation is only useful if the estimate is reliable for the individual patient, yet segmentation models are almost always compared with overlap metrics such as Dice. We ask whether lesion overlap actually certifies patient-level tumor burden, using 1,014 autoPET FDG PET/CT studies and 597 PSMA studies. Ranking five FDG models by Dice reproduces their ranking by MTV error, so overlap is adequate for model selection. At the patient level, however, the two criteria disagree: 82 of 489 patients (16.8%) fall on opposite sides of the Dice and MTV-error thresholds, and 236 of 489 patients have lesions that every model misses. We therefore recommend reporting patient-level MTV error and missed-lesion counts alongside Dice whenever automated MTV is intended for clinical use."
